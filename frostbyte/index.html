The Atelier

Three disciplines

01

Metalwork

Each shank is raised from white gold sheet and forged down over a steel mandrel — never cast from a master. The chrome bands are turned solid and hand-polished through six grades, which is why no two catch the light in quite the same way.

02

Stone setting

Rose quartz is cut as a dome and closed in a rub-over bezel, so the stone sits flush with the metal and takes the knocks. Topaz is claw-set high and left open underneath, so light passes through the pavilion and the pink stays alive against the white gold.

03

One of one

Pieces are made to order in a single size and finished by hand. Lead time is six weeks. The stone you are shown is the stone you receive — each is photographed and logged before it leaves the bench.
