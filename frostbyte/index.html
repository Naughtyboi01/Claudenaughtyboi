The Atelier

Three disciplines

01

Metalwork

Each shank is raised from sterling sheet and forged down over a steel mandrel — never cast from a master. Granulation is applied one bead at a time and fused, not soldered, which is why no two surfaces catch the light the same way.

02

Stone setting

Onyx is cut as a flat table and closed in a rub-over bezel, so the stone sits flush with the metal and takes the knocks. Garnet is claw-set high and left open underneath, so light passes through the pavilion and the red stays alive against the silver.

03

One of one

Pieces are made to order in a single size and finished by hand. Lead time is six weeks. The stone you are shown is the stone you receive — each is photographed and logged before it leaves the bench.
