Privacy.

The short version: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and nothing loaded from anyone else's server. The only personal data we hold is what you choose to send us.

Last updated 7 August 2026 · Naught to Ten, Mervue, Galway

Who we are

Naught to Ten is a web design studio at 14 Michael Collins, Mervue, Galway, Ireland, run by Joshua Naughton. For anything on this page, contact naughttoten@outlook.ie or call +353 89 447 0626. We are the data controller for any personal data described below.

What this website collects

Nothing, automatically. Specifically:

  • No cookies. The site sets none, of any kind, including for analytics or preferences. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom, no server-side tracking.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, images and video are served from this domain. Your browser does not contact any other company while loading these pages.
  • No advertising or profiling of any kind, and no automated decision-making.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs — an IP address, a timestamp, a requested page — as almost every web server does. Those exist for security and reliability, are not linked to you by us, and are not used for analytics.

The enquiry form

If you use the form on the contact page, it sends us the fields you filled in: your name, email address, and — if you chose to give them — company, phone number, budget range, timing and whatever you wrote in the message box.

Why: so that we can reply to you and, if it goes further, quote for and carry out the work. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, steps taken at your request before entering a contract.

Do you have to? No. The form is entirely optional, and you are as welcome to email or phone instead. Within the form, name and email are the only fields we need — without them we have no way to answer you.

The form is protected from spam by a hidden field that people never see and automated submitters usually fill in. We deliberately do not use a CAPTCHA, because that would load code from another company and send them your IP address.

Beyond those, we disclose personal data only where the law requires it, or where a professional adviser such as an accountant needs it.

Data sent outside the EU

Netlify is based in the United States, so enquiry submissions and server logs are processed there. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are permitted only with a recognised safeguard in place, and ours rests on Netlify's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework together with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in our data processing agreement with them.

If you would rather nothing of yours crossed the Atlantic, email or phone us directly instead of using the form — our mailbox is inside the EU.

How long we keep things

  • Form submissions are deleted from Netlify once they have reached our mailbox, and within three months at the latest.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted from our mailbox within twelve months.
  • Records connected to paid work are kept for six years, which is what Irish tax and limitation rules require.
  • Server logs are kept by Netlify to their own retention schedule, typically a matter of weeks.

When you email or call us

If you contact us directly we hold what you send — your name, contact details and whatever you tell us about the business — for the same purposes and for the same periods set out above. The lawful bases are our legitimate interest in responding to someone who has contacted us, and performance of a contract once one exists.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018 you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong, or complete it if it is missing something;
  • delete it;
  • restrict what we do with it, or object to us using it at all;
  • hand it over in a portable, machine-readable format.

Email naughttoten@outlook.ie and we will respond within one month. There is no charge. We are a small studio and are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer; Joshua Naughton handles these requests personally.

We do not make any decision about you by automated means, and we do not profile you.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Irish supervisory authority, the Data Protection Commission, at dataprotection.ie.

Other websites

Changes

If this notice changes — because we add a supplier, a tool, or anything that collects more than it does today — the date at the top of this page changes with it.
