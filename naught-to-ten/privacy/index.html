Privacy.

The short version: this website collects nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no requests to anyone else's server.

Last updated 7 August 2026 · Naught to Ten, Mervue, Galway

Who we are

Naught to Ten is a web design studio at 14 Michael Collins, Mervue, Galway, Ireland, run by Joshua Naughton. For anything on this page, contact naughttoten@outlook.ie or call +353 89 447 0626. We are the data controller for any personal data described below.

What this website collects

Nothing, automatically. Specifically:

  • No cookies. The site sets none, of any kind, including for analytics or preferences. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom, no server-side tracking.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, images and video are served from this domain. Your browser does not contact any other company while loading these pages.
  • No advertising or profiling of any kind, and no automated decision-making.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs — an IP address, a timestamp, a requested page — as almost every web server does. Those exist for security and reliability, are not linked to you by us, and are not used for analytics.

The enquiry form

The form on the contact page currently runs entirely in your browser. It validates what you type and shows a confirmation, and the details are not transmitted anywhere or stored. Nothing you type into it reaches us until the form is connected to a mail service, at which point this section will be updated to say exactly where the data goes and how long it is kept.

If you would like to reach us in the meantime, email or phone directly.

When you email or call us

If you contact us about a project, we hold what you send — your name, contact details and whatever you tell us about the business — so that we can reply and, if it goes further, quote for and carry out the work. The lawful bases are our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry, and performance of a contract once one exists.

Email sits in our Outlook mailbox, provided by Microsoft. Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within twelve months. Records connected to paid work are kept for six years, which is what Irish tax and limitation rules require.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except where a supplier is needed to deliver the work — for example a hosting provider — or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018 you can ask us to give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable format. Email naughttoten@outlook.ie and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Other websites

This site links out to Instagram. Once you follow a link you are on someone else's site, under their privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this notice changes — most likely when the enquiry form is connected to a real mail service — the date at the top of this page changes with it.
