These are the defaults. Anything in a signed proposal takes precedence over what follows.

Quotes and scope

Quotes are fixed against a written scope and hold for thirty days. Work outside that scope is quoted separately and agreed in writing before it starts — nothing gets added to an invoice as a surprise.
